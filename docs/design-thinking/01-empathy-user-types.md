# Empathy — User Types

## Definitions

**Experience level** — how much structured training background the user has:
- Beginner: Little to no structured training history; needs foundational guidance and close supervision.
- Intermediate: Has trained on and off, knows basic movements, but lacks consistency or advanced programming.
- Advanced: Extensive training history; needs programming and feedback more than instruction.

**Tech comfort** — how comfortable the user is navigating the app/tech independently:
- Low: Prefers calls or simple messages over app navigation; needs minimal steps, large text, guided walkthroughs.
- Medium: Comfortable with common apps; can follow structured flows without much hand-holding.
- High: Confident with software; will explore features independently and expects efficiency.

## 1. Family
- Relationship: Family
- Experience level: Beginner
- Goals: Stay healthy and mobile, manage weight, follow along without needing to think about programming
- Tech comfort: Low
- Session style: In-person or video call, slower pace, needs cues repeated and form corrected live

## 2. Close Friend
- Relationship: Friend / training buddy
- Experience level: Intermediate
- Goals: Get back into consistent training, wants accountability more than new knowledge
- Tech comfort: Medium — would use logging/reminders if convenient
- Session style: Remote, semi-independent — checks in for programming and form feedback, trains on own schedule

## 3. Paying Client
- Relationship: Client (no prior personal relationship)
- Experience level: Varies — onboarding needs to establish this per client
- Goals: Clear program, visible progress, confidence that they're getting professional value for payment
- Tech comfort: Unknown by default — app needs to work for a wide range without assuming familiarity
- Session style: Fully remote, expects structured check-ins, progress tracking, and clear communication of expectations/pricing

## 4. Injury / Rehab / Physical Limitation
- Relationship: Client, friend, or family member training around an injury or ongoing physical limitation
- Experience level: Varies — often Beginner to Intermediate
- Goals: Make safe progress without aggravating the limitation, regain function/confidence, avoid re-injury
- Tech comfort: Varies — no assumption either way
- Session style: Needs closer form review (e.g. video check-ins), modified/substituted exercises, frequent communication about pain or limits, and may require coordinating with the client's medical/physical therapy (PT) guidance

## 5. Coach / Me (Provider)
- Relationship: You — the person running and managing the service
- Experience level: Advanced
- Goals: See at a glance who hasn't checked in this week, review submitted form/videos efficiently, track each client's progress and flag who needs attention next
- Tech comfort: High
- Session style: Mostly asynchronous oversight (reviewing check-ins, videos, progress logs) punctuated by live coaching sessions; needs an overview, not a single linear session
